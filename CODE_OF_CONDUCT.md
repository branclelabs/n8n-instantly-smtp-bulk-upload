# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct v2.1 (Abridged)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior: empathy, respect for differing opinions, constructive feedback, responsibility, focus on community good.

Unacceptable: sexualized language, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private info without permission, other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement

Report to `security@example.com`. All complaints reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove or ban for violations.
